## Image fitting

Every replacement is cropped to the exact pixel dimensions its container expects,
scaled to cover first and cropped with an upward bias so faces are not cut.

`page-header/banner6.png` was re-cropped from 2178x1245 (ratio 1.75) to
2178x666 (ratio 3.27) to match the other page-header banners — its container is
about 2.98 wide, so the original was losing roughly 40% of the frame.
